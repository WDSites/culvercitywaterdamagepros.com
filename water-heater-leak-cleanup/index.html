How water heaters fail in Culver City homes

Tank heaters wear out from the inside, and a rusted tank or a corroded fitting can start as a drip and become a flood. Many Culver City heaters sit in a garage, a hallway closet or an attic, where a slow leak can run for days before anyone sees it.

State law adds another reason to pay attention. California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquakes. A heater that shifted in a quake can pull a rigid connection loose, and a heater that was never strapped is more exposed.

First hour safety steps for a leaking water heater

Hot water, electricity and gas can all be present, so take these in order.

  • Turn off the heater at its power switch or breaker, or set a gas unit to pilot or off.
  • Close the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the tank.
  • Do not touch the tank, the pipes or wet cords while standing in water.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911, because the city's fire department advises knowing how to shut off utilities if you smell gas.
  • Call a plumber for the heater and dispatch for the water.

Replacement, permits and who does what

The Building Safety Division's published list says a new, replaced or relocated water heater needs a plumbing permit. It also lists wiring for appliances such as water heaters among electrical permit items. Your plumber should explain which permits the work needs.

We do not replace water heaters. We remove the water, dry the floor and walls and clear wet materials. Keeping the two jobs separate also helps your insurer see what each invoice covers.

How the cleanup runs after a heater leak

A heater leak is often clean supply water, but it can still soak flooring and framing.

  1. Confirm the source is off. The crew checks that the supply is closed and power is off before any equipment is plugged in.
  2. Extract water. Wet vacuums and pumps pull standing water from concrete, tile or subfloor.
  3. Check the surroundings. Moisture meters scan the closet walls, the base of drywall and adjoining rooms.
  4. Remove soaked materials. Wet baseboard, insulation and carpet pad are removed when they will not dry in place.
  5. Dry and log. Air movers and a dehumidifier run while readings are recorded until the area is dry.

What changes the cost of a water heater leak cleanup

The main factors are how long the heater leaked, what the floor is made of and whether water crossed into living space. A leak onto a sealed garage slab is a smaller job than a leak under a bedroom wall.

Wet drywall, cabinets, carpet and insulation each add scope. The scope and price are explained on site before work begins.

Insurance and water heater leaks

A sudden tank failure is often treated as accidental water damage, and a leak that dripped for months is often treated as maintenance. Policies differ, and the cost of the heater itself may be treated separately from the cleanup.

Photograph the tank, the label and the water. Keep the plumber's invoice and our moisture readings, and give them to your insurer.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

A few habits prevent most heater floods.

  • Ignoring rust or moisture at the base of the tank.
  • Leaving the heater on after it starts leaking.
  • Storing cardboard and fabric against the heater.
  • Skipping a drain pan and a leak alarm in a closet above finished rooms.
  • Waiting for a failure when the heater is well past its expected age.
