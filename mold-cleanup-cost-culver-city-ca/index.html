How Much Does Mold Cleanup Cost in Culver City, CA?

The cost of mold cleanup depends on how much area is affected, which materials are involved, how much containment the job needs and whether testing and rebuilding are included. A web page cannot honestly state a price, because mold jobs range from a small, contained patch to an entire wall system. This guide explains what moves the cost and how to compare written scopes.

By the Culver City Water Damage Pros team · October 27, 2026 · Cost

Why mold prices vary so much

Mold is a symptom. The job depends on the water that fed it, how long the materials stayed wet and where the growth is. A small patch on a bathroom ceiling and a wall full of growth behind a shower are different projects, even if both look like a stain from the outside.

A quote that does not follow an inspection is a guess. A fair scope names the area, the materials, the containment, the testing and what is not included.

Six factors that drive the price

Ask each contractor how each factor applies to your home.

Affected area

More square footage means more containment, removal and cleaning.

Materials involved

Drywall and insulation are removed, while hard surfaces may be cleaned. Wood framing may be cleaned or replaced.

Containment level

Barriers, negative air and HEPA filtration add setup and equipment time.

Testing

Pre-remediation sampling and clearance testing are separate costs.

Drying and moisture repair

Fixing the water source and drying the structure are separate from removal.

Rebuild

New drywall, texture, paint and trim are usually a separate scope.

Testing costs and independent clearance

Testing can add cost, and it answers a different question from removal. Sampling shows what is present, and clearance testing checks results after remediation. An independent tester, one who did not do the cleanup, avoids a conflict of interest.

Lenders and buyers sometimes ask for clearance before a sale or loan closes. Nobody can promise how they will react, so ask them what they need before you order testing.

Costs outside the remediation itself

Many mold jobs include costs that are not in the remediation scope. A plumber or roofer fixes the water source, and a drying crew dries the structure. A repair crew then replaces drywall, flooring and finishes. These are often separate scopes and separate prices.

Ask each contractor which of these they include. The water damage repair page explains how rebuild scope and price are set, and water mitigation covers drying. Together they give you the full picture.

Insurance and mold cleanup

Coverage depends on the cause. Many policies cover mold that follows a covered sudden loss, and many exclude mold from long-term moisture. The California Department of Insurance says flood insurance typically does not cover mold or mildew damage that could have been avoided.

Ask your insurer before work begins, and keep photos, reports and invoices. State licensing rules for mold work vary, so ask whether your state licenses mold work before you hire anyone.

How to compare two mold quotes fairly

Put the two scopes side by side and check that they cover the same rooms, the same containment and the same exclusions. A lower number often leaves out testing, rebuild or disposal, and the difference shows up later as an add-on.

Ask both companies who will do the work, how they separate the work area and what they will do if they find more damage. A clear written answer is a better sign than a low price. Take your time, since mold is rarely an emergency that needs a same-hour decision.

Red flags in a mold quote

Slow down if you see any of these.

  • A price given with no inspection.
  • No mention of containment or HEPA filtration.
  • A promise that mold will never return.
  • Pressure to sign today.
  • A company that also does the testing and the cleanup with no separation.

Culver City resources before you sign

The Building Safety Division offers an online permit portal and virtual appointments for questions about permits and building codes, and its phone number is (310) 253-5800. Its permit page lists shower wall repair and wall replacement among work that needs a permit, which can affect the rebuild after mold removal.

Ask the division before the walls are opened. If your property is in Marina del Rey, ask Los Angeles County instead. See mold remediation for what the cleanup includes.
