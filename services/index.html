How the six services fit together

A water loss usually moves through phases. First the water has to be stopped and removed, then the structure dries, then ruined materials are cleaned out and replaced. Mold remediation is a separate track that sits alongside these phases, because mold only appears when something stays wet.

If water is moving right now, emergency water removal and water mitigation are the pages to read. If the water is gone but the room is damaged, water damage cleanup and water damage repair are the next step. Contaminated storm or sewage water is covered under flood damage restoration, and any dark growth or musty smell is covered under mold remediation.

How scope and price work across all six services

No service page can quote a number honestly, because water category, affected area, materials and drying time change the cost. For that reason every job starts with a visit, and the crew explains the scope and price on site before any work begins.

Ask for the scope in writing, ask what will be removed and why, and ask to see moisture readings while equipment runs. Those three questions work with any restoration company, and we welcome them.

What we do not do

We are a water damage restoration and mold remediation service. We do not repair plumbing lines, install waterproofing or fix foundations, and a plumber, roofer or HVAC technician is still needed to repair the source of a leak. We handle the water, the drying and the rebuild.

Fire and smoke restoration are not part of this service plan, and neither is basement waterproofing. If you are not sure whether your problem fits, call and ask.
