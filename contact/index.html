How to reach us

Phone is best. Call (424) 550-5662, any hour, any day. Dispatch answers the line, and calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

For non-urgent questions, email contact@culvercitywaterdamagepros.com. Email is not monitored for emergencies, so call if water is present. Our address is 6167 Bristol Pkwy, Culver City, CA 90230.

What to have ready when you call

A few details help dispatch decide what to send and how to advise you in the meantime.

  • Your address, including the neighborhood or cross street.
  • Where the water is and how far it has spread.
  • Whether the source is stopped, and where the shutoff valve is.
  • Whether the water is clear, gray or has an odor.
  • Whether anyone is at risk near electrical equipment.
  • Your insurance company name, if you plan to file a claim.

Hours

The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Crews are assigned the same day when one is available, and dispatch tells you plainly what to expect.
