Service Areas: Culver City and Marina del Rey, CA

Our dispatch line covers Culver City and the nearby Marina del Rey area in Los Angeles County. This page explains the coverage and how to confirm your address.

  • Dispatch open 24/7
  • Culver City and Marina del Rey

Culver City

Culver City is our home base, and our main ZIP codes there are 90230 and 90232. The city recognizes 15 neighborhoods, including Downtown Culver City, Blair Hills, Fox Hills, Lucerne-Higuera, Studio Village and Sunkist Park, and all of them fall inside our dispatch area. We also have pages for Culver Crest, Sunkist Park, Fox Hills and Downtown.

Crews reach the city by Interstate 405, Interstate 10 or State Route 90, depending on where they start. Tell dispatch the neighborhood, cross street or a nearby landmark, such as the Culver Hotel downtown or Culver City High School, and the crew plans the route.

Marina del Rey

Marina del Rey is an unincorporated community of Los Angeles County on the coast, west of Culver City. Our main ZIP code there is 90292, and ZIP code 90295 is also on our list. The Marina del Rey page explains dispatch, the county and district details that apply there and what to do before a crew arrives.

Nearby places

We also hear from people in neighboring parts of Los Angeles, such as Playa Vista, Mid-City and West Adams. Those places are not on our standard coverage list, so call and ask whether a crew can reach your address.

Dispatch will tell you honestly if a crew cannot reach you in a reasonable time, and when that happens it is better to hear it at once so you can call another company while the water is still small.

What to tell dispatch

A few details let dispatch confirm coverage and plan the route in one call.

  1. Your address and ZIP code. Give the street address, unit number and ZIP code so dispatch can check coverage.
  2. What is wet. Name the rooms, the floor level and whether the water is clean, gray or from a sewer.
  3. Whether water is still running. If it is, close the main shutoff valve and say where it is.
  4. Access details. Mention a gate code, a parking garage or a building manager, which is common in Marina del Rey.

Culver City offices worth knowing

Several city offices matter when water gets into a home. The Culver City Fire Department stores sandbags at its headquarters, 9600 Culver Blvd, and advises residents not to enter flooded subterranean garages. Public Works handles catch basins and can be reached at (310) 253-5635, and the Office of Emergency Management is at (310) 253-5900.

Golden State Water serves the Culver City system, and its customer line, 1-800-999-4033, is open around the clock. It maintains water lines only up to the meter, so a leak on your side is yours to fix. Call a plumber for the pipe, and call us for the water, the drying and the rebuild.

How coverage and permits differ by place

In Culver City, the Building Safety Division at City Hall, 9770 Culver Blvd, handles permits, and its phone number is (310) 253-5800. In Marina del Rey, the Los Angeles County Waterworks Districts operate the Marina del Rey water system for the county's Department of Beaches and Harbors, and the district lists a water emergency line at 1-800-675-HELP.

For permits in Marina del Rey, ask Los Angeles County which office covers your address, because the community is not a city.
