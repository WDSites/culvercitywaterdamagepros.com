Rain Water Damage Cleanup in Culver Crest, Culver City, CA

Where Culver Crest sits

Blanco-Culver Crest is one of the 15 neighborhoods on the City of Culver City's official neighborhoods map, and the part above Culver City's flat core is often called Upper Crest or Culver Crest. The hillside streets climb toward the Baldwin Hills side of the city, and the homes there are residential.

The city's neighborhood map is for representational purposes only, so treat its boundaries as approximate. If you are unsure whether your street is covered, ask dispatch.

What the city said in February 2024

On February 5, 2024 the City of Culver City asked residents above, below or beside a hillside in the Upper Crest area to take extra precautions because of continuing rain. The notice listed streets including Cranks Road, Flaxton Street, Youngworth Road and Tellefson Road, and said mud flows had been reported behind two homes on Cranks Road and one on Flaxton Street.

The city advised monitoring property for signs of landslides, consulting a geotechnical expert about hillside hazards and calling 911 for an immediate danger. We do not evaluate slopes or clear slides, and we do not claim indoor damage occurred in any specific home.

First hour safety steps

On a hillside, outdoor conditions are part of the hazard.

  • If the city has issued a warning for your street, follow it and call 911 for any immediate danger.
  • Stay out of rooms where water is dripping near light fixtures or outlets.
  • Do not go onto a wet roof or a muddy slope to look for the source.
  • Move valuables away from walls that are wet or leaning.
  • Photograph the damage from a safe spot and call dispatch.

How cleanup and drying work after rain intrusion

The crew follows the water from the opening to the last wet layer.

  1. Trace the path. The crew follows the stain upward and checks walls and ceilings below it.
  2. Extract and remove. Standing water is pumped out, and wet insulation and soft drywall are removed.
  3. Map moisture. Meters and a thermal camera show where framing and wall cavities hold water.
  4. Dry the structure. Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings reach dry values.
  5. Hand off the repair. Once dry, the opening is repaired by a roofer and the interior is rebuilt. See drywall water damage repair.

What changes the cost

Cost follows how much material got wet, not how big the opening was. Several storms feeding one hole can wet more insulation than one heavy night. Ceiling height, wall area and the number of drying days all change the scope.

A crew explains scope and price on site after reading the damage.

Insurance and storm damage

Insurers often separate sudden storm damage from long-term wear. Mud flows, landslides and earth movement are commonly treated differently from rain through a roof, and they may be excluded from a standard policy. Read yours and ask your insurer how each cause is classified.
